# Product

## Register

product

## Users

GovAI Hub serves internal AI-governance leads, risk and compliance teams, system owners, and governance approvers. They use it to keep risks, controls, supporting evidence, and human decisions in one shared record.

## Product Purpose

GovAI Hub is a French-first, bilingual workspace for the internal governance of AI systems. It helps teams register systems, assess risk, audit controls, manage corrective actions, record incidents, and retain the evidence behind each human decision.

## Brand Personality

Calm, rigorous, trustworthy. The product should feel clear and dependable for people reviewing consequential AI-system information.

## Anti-references

Avoid futuristic AI imagery, glossy or flashy visual effects, overly dense compliance dashboards, and intimidating legal-tech styling. Do not present the workspace as a legal authority or certification product.

## Design Principles

1. Make evidence easy to trace from risk to decision.
2. Keep human responsibility visible at every decision point.
3. Use calm hierarchy to make complex review work easier to scan.
4. Favor clear workflow guidance over decorative interface elements.
5. Give each role a shared, understandable record of the system under review.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, keyboard-first interaction, visible focus states, and reduced-motion support. Preserve clear French and English copy, semantic structure, and readable labels at zoom.
